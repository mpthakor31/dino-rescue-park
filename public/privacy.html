🦖 Dino Rescue Park – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

In short: Dino Rescue Park is a game made for children. We do not ask for, collect, or store any personal information. Game progress stays on your device. Ads are kid-safe, non-personalised and served by Google AdMob under its child-directed settings.

1. Information we collect

We (the developer) do not collect any personal information such as name, email, phone number, location, photos or contacts. The game does not have accounts, chat, or any way for children to share information with others.

The dinosaur names a child types in the game, coins, eggs and other progress are saved only on the device (local storage) and are never sent to us.

2. Advertising

The game shows ads using Google AdMob to keep the game free. All ad requests are configured for children:

  • Ad requests are tagged as child-directed (COPPA) and under the age of consent (GDPR).
  • Only non-personalised ads are requested; there is no interest-based advertising or remarketing.
  • The maximum ad content rating is set to G (General audiences).
  • The app does not use the Android Advertising ID.
  • We only use ad networks that are self-certified for Google Play's Families Policy.
  • Video ads that give rewards are always optional – the child chooses to watch them.

To deliver and measure ads, Google may process limited technical information (for example IP address, device type and app version) as described in Google's policy: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Permissions

The app only uses the Internet permission, which is needed to show ads. It does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or files.

4. Parents' area

Settings and external links are behind a parental gate (a simple maths question) so that children cannot open them by accident.

5. Children's privacy

We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR, and Google Play's Families Policy. If you believe a child's information has been collected, please contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Deleting data

Because data is only stored on the device, you can delete it at any time by using "Reset progress" in the Parents area, or by uninstalling the app.

7. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
